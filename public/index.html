FROM THE FEELING TO THE NEXT STEP

A feeling is part of your story.
It isn’t the whole story.

01 / NOTICE

Give it a little space.

Describe the moment and what you felt. You can be honest without judging yourself.

02 / SOFTEN

Find a fairer perspective.

Separate what happened from what self-doubt said it meant about you.

03 / MOVE

Choose a small next step.

You don’t need to erase the feeling to do something that matters to you.
